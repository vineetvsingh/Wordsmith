Wordsmith

How to play

Type a real English word and press Enter. Each tile then turns to show how close that letter is.

F O R G E
  • F is in the word, in the right spot.
  • O is in the word, but in another spot.
  • R, G and E aren’t in the word.

Every word is fetched online, and every guess is checked against a dictionary, so you’ll need a connection. The bulb reveals one letter ( per game). Change the length, guesses and mode in Settings.
